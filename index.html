Our Engineering Framework

Not every lab needs an expensive full rebuild. We systematically evaluate your space into three clear categories to preserve capital and prevent unnecessary disruption:

Category 01

RETAIN

Identify what still works. Solid worktops, sound utility runs, and compliant partitions are preserved, saving you capital and minimizing downtime.

Category 02

MODIFY

Re-engineer targeted elements: re-balance ACMV airflows, branch electrical sub-circuits, reposition furniture, and optimize bench workflows.

Category 03

REPLACE

Replace obsolete infrastructure that poses safety risks or restricts critical operations: worn fume extraction, non-compliant M&E, or failing surfaces.

Not Just Another Renovation Contractor

A lab isn’t an office fit-out. Standard contractors often tear out infrastructure you could have kept—or miss the power, airflow, and heat thresholds your instruments actually need. As lab engineers, we coordinate every detail in phases so your daily testing never has to stop.

Frequently asked questions

Q: Can we renovate our laboratory without completely rebuilding it?

Yes. Using our Retain / Modify / Replace framework, we systematically categorize what infrastructure remains technically sound and code-compliant. Many labs achieve their expansion and equipment integration goals with targeted M&E or ACMV re-balancing, avoiding full strip-out demolition costs.

Q: Can our laboratory continue operating during renovation?

Yes. We specialize in live, operational facilities. We develop phased renovation schedules, negative-pressure dust containment partitions, off-hours/weekend tie-ins, and temporary utility bypasses so mission-critical testing schedules proceed uninterrupted.

Q: We're purchasing new laboratory equipment — when should we involve AZIL?

Ideally before placing the purchase order or signing delivery contracts. We conduct a pre-arrival utility assessment to cross-check the manufacturer's cut-sheet against your site's electrical capacity, exhaust static pressure, cooling BTUs, gas purity, and floor vibration thresholds.

Q: Why has our laboratory become hotter after adding equipment?

Modern analytical instruments (LC-MS, mass spectrometers, incubators, robotic handlers) generate massive sensible heat loads. If your ACMV system was sized for simple bench testing, the air volume exchange is overwhelmed. We calculate thermal outputs and re-engineer airflow paths and localized exhaust to restore thermal balance.

Q: Can AZIL upgrade only our M&E or ventilation without renovating the entire laboratory?

Yes. We frequently execute targeted infrastructure packages—such as upgrading electrical sub-panels, adding dedicated clean power circuits, installing localized LEV fume arms, or replumbing drainage manifolds—without touching architectural furniture.

Q: What types of facilities does AZIL support for renovation?

We support active, operating research, testing, QC, and industrial laboratories. We maintain engineering teams and project managers delivering fit-out, renovation, M&E, and commissioning for mission-critical facilities.

Q: How do we know what actually needs upgrading?

That is precisely the purpose of our initial Laboratory Assessment. We inspect your physical site, review your equipment lists and workflows, run capacity checks on M&E and ACMV, and provide a clear Retain / Modify / Replace roadmap before you commit to construction budgets.
